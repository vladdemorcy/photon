Photon

Lights that follow what's playing. Your rooms dim when a movie starts, come up when you pause, and go back when it ends.

This installs:

  • Photon, running quietly in the background from when your Mac starts, even when nobody is logged in.
  • Its own copy of Node.js, downloaded from nodejs.org the first time (about 50 MB), so nothing else on your Mac is changed.
  • Photon in Applications, which opens Photon in your browser, and Uninstall Photon in Applications > Utilities.

Your rooms and settings are kept in /Library/Application Support/Photon. Updating never touches them: just run a newer installer.
