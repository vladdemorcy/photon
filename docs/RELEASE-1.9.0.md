Photon 1.9.0 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.9.0.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.9.0.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.9.0.apk`

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**New: Yeelight**
- Yeelight bulbs, light strips, lamps and ceiling lights, under Connections > Lights. Photon controls them directly on your network, with no cloud and no account.
- Turn on "LAN Control" for each light in the Yeelight app (open the light, tap the settings icon, then LAN Control). Photon then finds them by itself, or you can type a light's IP.
- Color bulbs and strips take colors and white, with smooth fades; white-only lights take brightness. When playback stops, each light goes back to how it was, color included.
- Like every other brand, Yeelights can share a room with Hue, Govee or any other lights.

**Also**
- Photon now has a LICENSE file: it's free to use at home, and the code is all rights reserved. The third-party components it includes, and their licenses, are listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md, which now ships with all three installers (the Mac installer was missing it).
- The Plex card now explains what to do with a free Plex account and no Plex Media Server: follow the TV under TVs and players instead.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
