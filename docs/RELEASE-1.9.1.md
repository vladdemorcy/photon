Photon 1.9.1 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.9.1.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.9.1.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.9.1.apk`

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**Gosund lights**
- Gosund bulbs, strips and plugs are Tuya devices, so they work through the Smart Life connection, the same one Treatlife uses. It's now called "Smart Life (Treatlife, Gosund)" under Connections > Lights.
- Lights still in the Gosund app need to be added to the Smart Life app first, since the Gosund app can't be linked to a Tuya cloud project. Reset each light (usually by switching it off and on three times until it blinks) and add it in Smart Life.

Everything else is the same as 1.9.0. See the [1.9.0 notes](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/releases/tag/v1.9.0) for Yeelight.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
