Photon 1.4.5 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.4.5.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.4.5.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.4.5.apk`, for a spare device that stays at home on Wi-Fi and plugged in, so no computer has to stay on.

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**What's in it**
- Lighting: Philips Hue, Govee, LIFX, WiZ, Nanoleaf, IKEA Home smart, Treatlife / Smart Life, Lutron Caseta and Home Assistant.
- Media: Plex (sign in with Plex, webhook set up for you), Jellyfin and Emby.
- Smart TVs: Roku, LG, Android TV / Fire TV, Chromecast, Vizio and Samsung, so lights follow any app on the TV.
- Rooms with 2D floor plans and 3D views, indoor and outdoor furniture, behaviors per event and time of day, random colors.
- Settings are kept separately from the program and are never touched by updates; saved versions let you roll back.

**Changes in 1.4.x**
- 1.4.5: the Photon banner on the welcome screen is now easy to read. It uses the same banner as the Windows installer.
- 1.4.4: a layout made for phones and touch tablets. Tabs along the bottom, rooms as a row you can swipe, one room section at a time, and lights, furniture and players that slide up from the bottom so you tap to add them instead of dragging across the page.
- Android app: runs Photon on a phone or tablet in the background, starts with the phone, and shows Photon's address in a notification.
- 1.4.3: remembers the TVs it finds, so they're picked up right away after a restart.
- 1.4.2: explains Roku's "Limited" mode on the TV card, with the steps to fix it.
- 1.4.1: each TV card shows what the TV reported on the last check.
- 1.4.0: Smart TVs.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
