Photon 1.6.1 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.6.1.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.6.1.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.6.1.apk`

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**Fixed**
- On phones, turning on a connection with a long button label (Lutron Caseta, IKEA Home smart, Hue Sync Box) made the Connections page wider than the screen, so it scrolled sideways. Button labels, messages and addresses in the connection cards now wrap.

Everything else is the same as 1.6.0. See the [1.6.0 notes](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/releases/tag/v1.6.0) for TV sync boxes.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
