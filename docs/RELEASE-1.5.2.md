Photon 1.5.2 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.5.2.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.5.2.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.5.2.apk`

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**What's new**
- Android app: the tab bar now sits right above the phone's navigation bar, and the header right below the status bar. On newer Android versions both left a gap the size of the phone's own bars, so less of the page fit on the screen.

Photon in a phone's browser and on Windows and Mac works the same as 1.5.1. See the [1.5.1 notes](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/releases/tag/v1.5.1) for the Settings tab and the [1.5.0 notes](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/releases/tag/v1.5.0) for sign-in and security.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
