Photon 1.7.0 for Windows, Mac and Android.

**Downloads**
- **Windows 10/11:** `Photon-Setup-1.7.0.exe`
- **macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Photon-1.7.0.pkg`
- **Android 8+ phone or tablet:** `Photon-1.7.0.apk`

See [Installing Photon](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/vladdemorcy/photon/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**New: Kodi**
- Kodi now works as a player, along with boxes that run it: LibreELEC, OSMC, CoreELEC, and Kodi on a Shield or Fire TV. Turn it on under Connections > TVs and players, then pick it as a room's player.
- Photon sees play, pause, resume and stop for anything Kodi plays, whether it comes from your library or an add-on, along with the title and whether it's a movie, an episode or music. Rules that only react to movies or only to music work with Kodi too.
- Setup in Kodi: Settings > Services > Control, turn on "Allow remote control via HTTP" and set a username and password, then enter the same ones in Photon. Photon finds Kodi by itself when "Announce services to other systems" is on (Settings > Services > General), or you can type its IP address.
- The Smart TVs section is now called TVs and players, since it's no longer only TVs.

The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask once before running them. The Android app isn't from the Play Store, so Android asks to allow installs from your browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
